## Big Listening Inquiry

**Research Question (Why):**
How do Bolivian national media, the mining sector, and Indigenous organisations frame lithium extraction in the salt flats (Salar de Uyuni, Coipasa, Pastos Grandes) and the rights of the communities living there, and whose framing dominates public conversation?

**What — Themes and Keywords**

- Indigenous and campesino organisations in Potosí and Oruro (e.g. FRUTCAS, Nor Lípez community organisations)
- Environmental and research NGOs (e.g. CEDIB, Fundación Solón, CEDLA)

Communities to locate:
- A state-development community framing lithium as sovereignty and progress
